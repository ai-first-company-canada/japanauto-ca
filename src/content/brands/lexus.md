## Why a used Lexus makes sense in Canada

People who lease an Audi A4, BMW 3 Series or Mercedes C-Class for three years and then swap mostly dodge the depreciation hit. The second owner, who keeps the car past warranty, pays the repair bills, and those run roughly two to three times a year what a Lexus IS or ES of the same age costs. Put a used 2019 Lexus IS 350 next to a used 2019 BMW 330i and the Lexus wins easily. The Lexus, on a five-year ownership horizon past warranty, costs less to operate than a Toyota Camry XLE.

Buyers know it. Lexus Certified Pre-Owned cars sell at a premium in Toronto, Vancouver and Montreal, and used Lexus stock turns over faster than German cars at the same price. Underneath, a Lexus is Toyota hardware. The RX is assembled at Toyota's Cambridge, Ontario plant alongside the Toyota RAV4. Shared Toyota powertrains explain most of Lexus's J.D. Power record, and the brand has ranked first or near the top of North American dependability studies for over a decade.

## Most popular used Lexus models

Ask a Canadian to picture a used Lexus and they'll picture an RX. A 2018 to 2022 RX 350 with 60,000 to 110,000 km in Toronto, Vancouver, or the Calgary luxury suburbs lists between $36,000 and $52,000. The RX 450h hybrid commands a $2,000 to $4,000 premium over the gas equivalent, and the F Sport trim adds another $3,000 over standard.

The IS is the entry sedan and the used-market rival to the BMW 3 Series and Audi A4. A 2019-2022 IS 350 AWD F Sport lists in the high $30,000s in Toronto and Vancouver, well under a comparable BMW 330i xDrive M Sport. The ES is a softer mid-size sedan on the Toyota Camry platform. The GS played the sporty role until Lexus dropped it. The NX is the smaller crossover, up against the Audi Q5 and BMW X3, and the LX is a body-on-frame SUV built on the Land Cruiser.

## Lexus vs German luxury reliability

The reliability gap is measurable. The J.D. Power Vehicle Dependability Study, which measures problems per 100 vehicles after three years of ownership, has placed Lexus first or near-first in North America for more than a decade. BMW, Audi, and Mercedes typically run 30 to 60 percent more problems per 100 vehicles in the same study window.

Most of the reason is that Lexus borrows its mechanicals from mainstream Toyotas. The 3.5L 2GR-series V6 fitted to the RX 350, ES 350, and IS 350 is the same engine family as the Toyota Camry XSE, Sienna, Avalon and Highlander, an engine family with billions of kilometres on Canadian roads. The [VVT-i](/glossary/vvt-i/) and Dual VVT-i variable valve systems, the Aisin-built automatic transmissions, and the [e-CVT](/glossary/e-cvt/) hybrid power-split devices in the RX 450h and ES 300h are just as durable in a Lexus as in a Toyota.

The Germans take more engineering risks. Think direct-injected turbo fours with complicated cooling, dual-clutch gearboxes whose mechatronic units fail at 100,000 km, and electrical systems with known faults that never got fully fixed. The cars are often more capable for it. They also cost more to keep running.

## Lexus hybrids in Canada

Lexus offers hybrid trims on RX (450h), NX (450h+ PHEV and 350h), ES (300h), UX (250h), and LS (500h). All of them use Toyota Hybrid Synergy Drive hardware: the [e-CVT](/glossary/e-cvt/) planetary power-split device, an Atkinson-cycle gas engine, and a nickel-metal-hydride or lithium-ion traction battery. Winter fuel penalties match the Toyota versions (15 to 25 percent worse than the rating at -20°C), and the cold does no harm to the system.

The plug-in versions (NX 450h+ and RX 450h+) don't help a used buyer with federal money. The old iZEV rebate closed in January 2025, and the Electric Vehicle Affordability Program that replaced it in February 2026 covers only new vehicles with a final price of $50,000 or less (the cap doesn't apply to Canadian-built EVs), which leaves out most Lexus plug-ins. The hybrid battery warranty follows Toyota Canada's schedule: 10 years or 240,000 km on 2020 and newer models, 8 years or 160,000 km on earlier ones.

## Lexus parts and certified service

Lexus Certified Pre-Owned cars come through the Lexus dealer network with a 1-year / 20,000 km powertrain warranty and roadside assistance. Longer extended coverage is sold separately. CPO usually adds $2,000 to $4,000 over a similar private sale, so price the extended plan you'd actually want before deciding the premium is worth it. Parts are easy to get, because most are Toyota parts moving through the same Canadian distribution. A shop that works on Toyotas will usually take a Lexus too, at about half the dealer labour rate. ## Common questions

### Is Lexus more reliable than BMW or Mercedes in Canada?

Yes, and the gap is large. The J.D. Power Vehicle Dependability Study has placed Lexus first or near-first in North America for more than a decade. BMW, Audi, and Mercedes typically run 30 to 60 percent more problems per 100 vehicles in the same three-year window. The reason is Toyota parts. The RX 350 uses the same 3.5L 2GR-family V6 as the Toyota Camry XSE, an engine with billions of kilometres on Canadian roads. German brands try more new technology on each model, which makes for more capable cars that break more often.

### Is a used Lexus RX a good buy?

Yes. With verified service history, a 2018 to 2022 RX 350 is one of the most reliable luxury crossovers you can buy, and used pricing in Toronto and Vancouver typically lands $4,000 to $8,000 below a comparable BMW X3 or Audi Q5 at the same age and kilometres. The RX 450h hybrid trim adds approximately $2,000 to $4,000 to used pricing and saves real fuel in city driving. Because it's built in Cambridge, Ontario, parts and warranty work are simpler than with a German import.

### How long does a Lexus engine last?

A well-maintained 3.5L 2GR-series V6 (RX 350, ES 350, IS 350, GS 350 and others) routinely exceeds 350,000 kilometres in Canadian service. The 2.5L A25A four-cylinder in the ES 250, shared with the Toyota Camry, has the same durability profile. The hybrid [e-CVT](/glossary/e-cvt/) has fewer moving parts than a regular automatic, and Toyota Prius taxis have run them past 500,000 km. None of that helps if the oil changes get skipped.

### Lexus or Acura: which luxury brand is better in Canada?

For most buyers, Lexus. It has the nicer cabin, more hybrids, and a measurably better dependability record in J.D. Power studies. [Acura](/brands/acura/) has the stronger AWD architecture in [SH-AWD](/glossary/awd/) and the Honda-shared parts pricing advantage. Lexus is the safer bet on long-term ownership cost. Choose Acura if you want sharper handling and AWD and cheaper parts down the road.

### Is a Lexus hybrid worth the price premium?

If you drive a lot in Toronto, Vancouver or Montreal, yes. The fuel economy advantage on a RX 450h or ES 300h versus the gas equivalent typically pays back the $2,000 to $4,000 used-purchase premium within 80,000 to 120,000 km of metropolitan driving. The [e-CVT](/glossary/e-cvt/) also outlasts a conventional automatic. If you drive little, mostly on rural highways, or sell after three or four years, buy the gas version.

### Which used Lexus holds its value best in Canada?

The RX, especially the 450h hybrid. Three-year retention on a RX 450h in Canadian Black Book averages typically lands around 65 to 70 percent of original MSRP. That's close to a Toyota RAV4 and well ahead of any German rival. The IS 350 AWD and NX 450h+ also retain well. The ES 300h, GX, and LX are more variable depending on trim and provincial market conditions.

### Are Lexus parts expensive in Canada?

Less than people expect. Parts shared with Toyota (engine, transmission, brakes, suspension) cost about the same at a Lexus counter as at a Toyota one, and Denso, Aisin, NGK or KYB equivalents from PartsAvatar.ca and PartsEngine.ca run 40 to 60 percent of dealer price. Lexus-only parts such as leather, interior trim, exterior lighting and infotainment cost more, though usually still less than BMW or Mercedes equivalents.
